Why my company is called Prokriti

When I registered my own company, I never had to think about the name. Prokriti is my daughter's name.

Within a few weeks of each other, I lost my father and became a father myself. Grief and joy arrived almost together. One day I was coming to terms with him being gone; soon after, I was holding a tiny new life. It was the most complex period of my life, and I am still learning how to hold both feelings at the same time.

He never got to meet her. But he knew she was coming, he knew she would be a girl, and he knew the name we had in mind: Prokriti. We made it final. In a way, her name carries a little of him too.

In Bengali, prokriti (প্রকৃতি) means nature: things that grow in their own way, in their own time. That felt right for a child, and right for something I am building from scratch. Putting her name on my company is my way of remembering why I work: for the people at home.
